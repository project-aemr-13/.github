# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free environment for everyone contributing to
NextProject13 projects.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate in all interactions.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the team.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

Report concerns to **[tech@project13.ro](mailto:tech@project13.ro)**. Maintainers are
responsible for clarifying standards and may take appropriate corrective action, including
removing contributions or restricting participation.

This policy is adapted in spirit from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
